What the app is

Quran Widget shows an ayah of the day with translation, hadiths, prayer times for your location, a qibla compass, a tasbih counter, the 99 names of Allah and home-screen widgets. It has no accounts and no sign-in. The app is offered as is: we keep improving it, but we do not promise that every feature will always be available or work without interruption.
