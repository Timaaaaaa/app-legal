Privacy Policy

Push Up Counter: 100 Pushups · Android · Last updated November 15, 2026

Push Up Counter counts your push-ups and keeps your workout history. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. Your workouts stay on your phone.

Camera

In camera mode the app uses the front camera to detect the position of your shoulders, elbows and wrists and count repetitions. Camera frames are analysed on your device in real time by an on-device pose detection model (Google ML Kit). Frames are not recorded, not saved to storage and not sent to us or to anyone else. The camera is used only while the training screen is open in camera mode.

You can decline the camera permission and count push-ups in touch mode instead. You can revoke the permission at any time in Android settings.

Your workouts stay on your device

Sessions (date and time, number of push-ups, duration, counting mode), your daily goal, streak and training plan progress are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see them. The home-screen widget reads today's count, your goal and streak from the same private storage.

Notifications

If you turn on the daily reminder, the app schedules one local notification a day on your device. No push service is used.

Usage analytics

We use Amplitude to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Amplitude receives only product events, never camera images:

  • In-app actions: onboarding steps, that a session was started or finished (with its counting mode, number of push-ups and duration as numbers), that the daily goal was reached, training plan workouts, and settings you change.
  • Premium activity: when the Premium screen is shown or closed, which plan you choose, and whether a purchase, trial, cancellation or restore happened, with the product identifier.
  • Device information sent with every event: a random device identifier, device model, Android version, app version, language, and the approximate location (country and city) derived by Amplitude from the network address.

We never set a user ID and never use the Android advertising ID. This data is not linked to your name, email or Google account, and it is not used for advertising.

On-device machine learning

Pose detection runs on your device. The Google ML Kit library may send Google limited diagnostic and usage metrics about the library itself (such as device information, app information and performance data), as described in Google's ML Kit terms. This does not include your camera images.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active on your device. RevenueCat receives your purchase history for this app and a random app user identifier, not your name or payment details.

Feedback

If you write to us from the app, we receive the text you type, the app version and device model so that we can reply. Nothing from your workouts is attached unless you write it yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Data on your phone. Your workouts, goal, streak and plan progress never leave your device. To delete them, open Android Settings → Apps → Push Up Counter → Storage → Clear data, or uninstall the app. They are deleted immediately.

Analytics data (Amplitude) and purchase records (RevenueCat). To request deletion:

  1. In the app, open Settings → “Write to us”, or email tolegenovtbdev@gmail.com.
  2. Write “Delete my data — Push Up Counter”.
  3. We delete the analytics events and the RevenueCat customer record linked to your device within 30 days and confirm by reply.

Google Play keeps its own record of purchases and subscriptions under Google's policies; we cannot delete it. Analytics events that are not deleted on request are kept for no longer than 12 months.
