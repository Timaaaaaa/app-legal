Push Up Counter: 100 Pushups · Android

The camera does not count my push-ups

Stand the phone on the floor about 1.5–2 metres away so that your shoulders, elbows and hands are in view. Good light helps. Go all the way down and fully straighten your arms: a rep is counted when your arms are straight again. If the hint “Get in position” stays on screen, the app cannot see your arms.

Touch mode

Switch to “Touch” on the training screen, put the phone on the floor under your chest and touch the screen with your nose or chest on every push-up. No camera is needed.

Daily goal and streak

Set the goal in Settings. A day counts toward your streak when you reach the goal that day.

Reminder and widget

Turn on the daily reminder in Settings and choose the time. Add the widget from Settings → “Add home screen widget”, or long-press the home screen → Widgets → Push Up Counter.

Premium and purchases

Manage or cancel a subscription in Google Play → Payments and subscriptions. If Premium does not appear after a purchase, open Settings → “Restore purchases”.
