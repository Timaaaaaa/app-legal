Privacy Policy

Tarot: Daily Card & Meanings · Last updated September 16, 2026

Daily Tarot shows a tarot card of the day on your home screen. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. This policy explains what the app does with data.

We collect nothing

This version of the app sends no data to us or to anyone else. There is no analytics service in it, no crash reporting and no advertising SDK. We have no servers that receive anything from your phone, and we cannot tell who has installed the app or what they do in it.

What stays on your device

Your language, the card of the day and your draws, your reading history, saved cards, chosen focus, background and widget settings are stored only on your device, in the app's private storage. Deleting the app deletes all of it.

Internet access

The app asks Android for internet access, but the cards, their meanings and the backgrounds all ship inside the app and are read from your device. Everything works with the phone offline.

Purchases

This version offers nothing to buy. When premium features are added, purchases will be processed by Google Play, we will never see your payment details, and this policy will be updated before that version is released.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not use the advertising ID.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or photos.

Children

The app is not directed at children and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Entertainment only

Tarot readings in this app are for reflection and entertainment. They are not advice — medical, legal, financial or otherwise.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
