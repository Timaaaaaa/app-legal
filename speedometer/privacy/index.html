Privacy Policy

Offline Speedometer & Odometer · Android · Last updated October 27, 2026

GPS Speedometer shows your speed and records your trips. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. Everything it measures stays on your phone.

Location

The app reads your device's GPS position only while the app is open on screen, and only to compute speed, distance, altitude and heading. Location is processed on the device and is never sent to us or to anyone else. There is no background location tracking: when you leave the app, GPS updates stop.

You can decline the location permission; the app will then show no speed. You can revoke it at any time in Android settings.

Your trips stay on your device

Trips (start and end time, distance, maximum and average speed, time over your limit) and their routes are stored in the app's private storage on your phone. We cannot see them. The home-screen widget reads a small summary (distance this month and the last trip) from the same private storage.

Export and sharing

When you share a trip summary or export a GPX or CSV file, the file is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (messenger, email, drive, a fitness service). Where it goes from there is governed by that app's policy.

Usage analytics

We may use Amplitude to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Amplitude receives only product events, never your location, routes or trips:

  • In-app actions: onboarding steps, that a trip was started or finished (with its distance, duration and maximum speed as numbers, not where it was), that a speed limit was set, which unit or digit colour was chosen, and the GPS state (searching, locked, no permission).
  • Premium activity: when the Premium screen is shown or closed, which plan you choose, and whether a purchase, trial, cancellation or restore happened, with the product identifier.
  • Device information sent with every event: a random device identifier, device model, Android version, app version, language, and the approximate location (country and city) derived by Amplitude from the network address, not from GPS.

We never set a user ID and never use the Android advertising ID. This data is not linked to your name, email or Google account, and it is not used for advertising.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active on your device. RevenueCat receives your purchase token and a random app user identifier, not your name or payment details.

Messages to the developer

If you use “Write to us”, your message, the optional contact you type, the app version, device model and language are sent to us so we can reply. Nothing from your trips is attached unless you paste it yourself.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Delete any trip from its page, or uninstall the app to remove all trips from your device. To delete analytics data tied to your device identifier, write to us at the address below.
