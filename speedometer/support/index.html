Support

Offline Speedometer & Odometer · Android

Speed shows 0 or “Searching for GPS”

  • Allow location for the app and make sure Location is on in Android settings. The app uses GPS only, so it needs a view of the sky: it may take 10–60 seconds to lock indoors or in a parking garage.
  • While you stand still the app shows an honest 0 instead of GPS noise. Speed appears once you move faster than about 3 km/h.
  • Speed is measured only while the app is open on screen. Keep it in the foreground on a dashboard mount; the screen stays on by itself.

Trips

  • Tap “Start trip”, or turn on “Auto-start trips” in Settings and recording begins by itself when you move.
  • If the phone dies or Android kills the app mid-trip, the unfinished trip is saved from the last recorded point when you open the app again.
  • Each trip keeps distance, time, moving time, maximum and average speed, time over your limit, and the route.

Speed limit alert

Tap the limit chip on the speed screen. A double beep and a vibration sound when you exceed the value you set. The limit is yours; the app does not know road signs.

HUD mode

Lay the phone on the dashboard at night: the digits are flipped so that their reflection in the windshield reads correctly. Tap the screen to exit.

Widget

Settings → “Add home screen widget”, or long-press the home screen → Widgets → GPS Speedometer. The widget shows distance this month, your last trip, and starts a trip in one tap.

Premium

Premium unlocks the unlimited trip journal, GPX and CSV export, all-time statistics and extra digit colours. If you already paid and Premium shows as off, open Settings → “Restore purchases” on the same Google account you used to buy.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes the trips. Export them first from Trips → Export CSV, or a single route from the trip page → Export GPX.
