Drive safely

The app measures speed from GPS and can be less accurate than your vehicle's instruments, especially in tunnels, between tall buildings or with a weak signal. The speed limit alert is a value you set yourself; it does not know the legal limit of the road you are on. Always follow your vehicle's speedometer, road signs and traffic law, and never operate the phone while driving.

Premium

Premium unlocks the unlimited trip journal, GPX and CSV export, all-time statistics and extra digit colours. It is sold as a monthly or yearly subscription, or as a one-time lifetime purchase, through Google Play. Subscriptions renew automatically until cancelled in your Google Play account; a free trial, where offered, converts to a paid subscription unless cancelled before it ends. Refunds are handled under Google Play's policy.
