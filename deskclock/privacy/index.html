Privacy Policy

Depth Clock Live Wallpaper · Android · Last updated November 24, 2026

Depth Clock Live Wallpaper puts a big clock on your home screen, behind the person, pet or object in your photo. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. Your photos never leave your phone.

Your photos

When you pick a photo, the app reads only that photo through the Android photo picker. It does not ask for access to your whole gallery. To place the clock behind the subject, the app separates the subject from the background with Google ML Kit subject segmentation, which runs on your device through Google Play services. The photo is not uploaded to us or to Google for this. Google Play services may download the segmentation model once; that download contains no photo.

The chosen photo, the cut-out subject and your clock settings are stored in the app's private storage on your phone so the wallpaper can be drawn. We cannot see them. Uninstalling the app deletes them.

Wallpaper and widget

The app uses the Android wallpaper permission only to set the wallpaper you choose. The home-screen clock widget and the minute tick use Android's alarm and boot permissions only to keep the time on the widget up to date after a restart. Nothing is sent anywhere for this.

Ready-made wallpapers

Ready-made wallpapers are public-domain or CC0 photos from Wikimedia Commons and are included in the app.

Usage analytics

We use Amplitude to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Amplitude receives only product events, never your photos:

  • In-app actions: onboarding steps, which clock face, colour or wallpaper was chosen, that a wallpaper was set, and whether the depth effect worked.
  • Premium activity: when the Premium screen is shown or closed, which option you choose, and whether a purchase, cancellation or restore happened, with the product identifier.
  • Device information sent with every event: a random device identifier, device model, Android version, app version, language, and the approximate location (country and city) derived by Amplitude from the network address.

We never set a user ID and never use the Android advertising ID. This data is not linked to your name, email or Google account, and it is not used for advertising.

Purchases

Purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active on your device. RevenueCat receives your purchase token and a random app user identifier, not your name or payment details.

Messages to the developer

If you use “Write to us”, your message, the optional contact you type, the app version, device model and language are sent to us so we can reply. No photo is attached.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Uninstall the app to remove your photos, cut-outs and settings from your device. To delete analytics data tied to your device identifier, write to us and we will remove it.
