AC Remote Control · Android

The AC doesn't respond

  • Check that your phone has an IR blaster: a small dark window on the top edge (most Xiaomi, Redmi and Poco phones). Samsung Galaxy phones don't have one.
  • Point the top edge of the phone at the indoor unit from within 5 meters.
  • Try the next remote in the list. If none of your brand's remotes work, choose “Try all remotes”: many ACs use another maker's remote.

Subscriptions

Manage or cancel your subscription in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. To restore Premium on a new phone, open Settings → Restore Purchases with the same Google account.
