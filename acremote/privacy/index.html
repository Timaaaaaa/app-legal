Privacy Policy

AC Remote Control · Android · Last updated October 22, 2026

AC Remote Control turns the infrared (IR) blaster of your phone into a remote for your air conditioner. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. This policy explains the small amount of data the app handles and why.

Infrared commands

Commands go from your phone's IR blaster straight to your air conditioner, like a regular remote. They never pass through the internet or any server.

What stays on your device

The air conditioners you add — their names, brands, remote types and last settings — and your preferences (°C or °F, haptics) are stored only on your device. Android may include them in your own device backup. We never receive them.

Usage analytics

We use Amplitude to understand how the app is used and improve it. Amplitude receives:

  • In-app actions: onboarding steps, which AC brand you choose, which remote types you try and which one works, how many times each button (power, temperature, mode, fan, swing) was pressed in a session, and whether the IR blaster failed to send.
  • Premium activity: when the Premium screen is shown or closed, which plan you choose, and whether a purchase, trial, cancellation or restore happened, with the product name.
  • Device information sent with every event: a random device identifier, device model, Android version, app version, language, and the approximate location (country, region and city) that Amplitude derives from the IP address of the request.

We never set a user ID and never use the Android advertising ID. This data is not linked to your name, email or Google account, and it is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active on your device. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier. We never see your payment details.

Messages to the developer

If you send us a message from the app's Support screen, we receive the text you type, the app version, your device model and language, so that we can answer. Don't include personal information you don't want us to have.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your name, contacts, photos or precise location.
