The app

AC Remote Control sends infrared commands from your phone's built-in IR blaster to your air conditioner. It works only on phones with an IR blaster. Remote types are matched by trying them on your air conditioner; we cannot guarantee that every model of every brand responds, or responds to every function.

Premium

AC Remote Premium unlocks the remote. It is offered as a weekly subscription with a free trial, a yearly subscription, or a one-time lifetime purchase. Prices are shown in the app before you buy. Payment is charged to your Google Play account. Subscriptions renew automatically at the end of each period unless cancelled at least 24 hours before it ends. If you cancel during a free trial, you are not charged. You can manage and cancel subscriptions in Google Play → Payments & subscriptions. Refunds are handled by Google Play under its policies.
