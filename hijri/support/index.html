Support

Hijri Calendar & Date Widget · Android

A question, a wrong date or a suggestion? Write to us: tolegenovtbdev@gmail.com. If a date is wrong, tell us your country and the date you expected.

The date differs from my country by a day

The app uses the Umm al-Qura calendar. Many countries start the month by moon sighting, so the date can differ by one or two days. Open Settings → Hijri date adjustment and shift the date by −2…+2 days. Widgets, reminders and the lock-screen date follow the same adjustment.

Adding a widget

In the app open Settings → Widgets and tap Add. Or long-press an empty spot on your home screen, choose Widgets and find “Hijri Calendar”.

The widget did not change at midnight

Android may delay the update by a few minutes to save battery. If it stays on yesterday's date, open the app once; please also check that battery optimisation is not restricting the app.

Premium

The calendar, events, converter, basic widgets and main reminders are free. Premium adds the month-calendar widget, more widget themes and reminders for every event. To restore a purchase: Settings → Restore purchases. To cancel a subscription: Google Play → Profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

Delete your data

Hijri Calendar & Date Widget has no accounts. Your settings, date adjustment and reminders are stored only on your phone.

  1. To delete everything stored on your phone, uninstall the app (or open Android Settings → Apps → Hijri Calendar → Storage → Clear data).
  2. To delete the usage analytics linked to your device, email tolegenovtbdev@gmail.com with the subject “Hijri Calendar data deletion”. If possible, include the date you installed the app.
  3. We delete the analytics events for your device within 30 days and confirm by email.

Purchase records are kept by Google Play and RevenueCat as required for billing and tax purposes; we cannot delete them, but they are not linked to your name by us.
