What the app is

Hijri Calendar & Date Widget shows the Islamic (Hijri) date calculated with the Umm al-Qura calendar, an Islamic month calendar, Islamic events, a date converter, reminders and home-screen widgets. Dates of Islamic months may differ from moon sighting in your country; the app offers a manual adjustment, and religious observances should follow your local authorities.
