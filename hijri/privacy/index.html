Privacy Policy

Hijri Calendar & Date Widget · Android · Last updated November 15, 2026

Hijri Calendar & Date Widget shows the Islamic (Hijri) date, an Islamic month calendar, Islamic events with countdowns, a date converter, reminders and home-screen widgets. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. This policy explains the small amount of data the app handles and why.

Everything is calculated on your device

Hijri dates are calculated on your device with the Umm al-Qura calendar. The app does not use your location and does not need an internet connection for the calendar, widgets or reminders.

What stays on your device

Your language, date adjustment, first day of the week, digit style, widget themes and reminder settings are stored only on your device and shared only with the app's own widgets on the same device. We never receive them.

Usage analytics

We use Amplitude to understand how the app is used and improve it. Amplitude receives:

  • In-app actions: onboarding steps, the interface language, which screens you open, that the date adjustment was changed and to which value, that the converter was used, which event you open, which kinds of widgets are installed and which theme they use, and which reminders are switched on.
  • Premium activity: when the Premium screen is shown or closed, which plan you tap, and whether a purchase, trial, cancellation or restore happened, with the product name. Whether Premium is active.
  • That the system rating prompt was shown (not your rating — Google does not share it).
  • Device information sent with every event: a random device identifier, device model, Android version, app version, language and the country.

We have turned off city, region, IP address and carrier collection. We never set a user ID, never use the advertising identifier (Android advertising ID) and never send anything you type. This data is not linked to your name, email or Google account, and it is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active on your device. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier. We never see your payment details.

Reminders and the lock-screen date

Event reminders and the optional daily Hijri date notification are local notifications scheduled on your device. No server is involved.

What we do not do

  • We do not sell or share your data with advertisers or data brokers.
  • We do not track you across other apps or websites.
  • We do not collect your name, email address, contacts, location or photos.

Third-party services

  • Amplitude Privacy Notice
  • RevenueCat Privacy Policy
  • Google Privacy Policy

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Your choices

You can turn reminders and the lock-screen date off at any time in the app's settings. You can delete all data stored on your device by deleting the app. To ask us to delete analytics data related to your device, email us at the address below.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date at the top of this page.
