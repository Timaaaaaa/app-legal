Privacy Policy

Migraine Tracker: Headache Log · Android · Last updated October 27, 2026

Migraine Tracker is a personal headache diary. It has no accounts, no sign-in and no advertising. What you write in it is personal, so this policy is deliberately short and specific about what the app does with it.

Your diary stays on your device

Everything you log — attacks, their time and duration, pain intensity and location, what you felt, possible triggers, free notes, and missed work or plans — is stored only in the app's private storage on your phone. We do not have a server, and we never receive your diary. Android may include it in your own device backup if you have backups enabled; that backup belongs to your Google account, not to us.

The home-screen widget reads a small summary (the date of your last attack) from the same private storage.

Report and export

When you share a report or export a CSV, the file is created on your device and handed to the app you choose (messenger, email, drive). Where it goes from there is governed by that app's policy. We never see it.

Usage analytics

We may use Amplitude to understand how the app is used and to improve it. Amplitude receives only product events, never the content of your diary:

  • In-app actions: onboarding steps, that an attack was logged or ended (with intensity and the number of symptoms, triggers and notes added — not which ones), that a report was shared, that the reminder was turned on or off, and which screen was opened.
  • Premium activity: when the Premium screen is shown or closed, which plan you choose, and whether a purchase, trial, cancellation or restore happened, with the product name.
  • Device information sent with every event: a random device identifier, device model, Android version, app version, language, and the approximate location (country, region and city) that Amplitude derives from the IP address of the request.

We never set a user ID and never use the Android advertising ID. This data is not linked to your name, email or Google account, and it is not used for advertising or tracking across other companies' apps.

Purchases

Subscriptions and purchases are processed by Google Play. We use RevenueCat to check whether Premium is active on your device. RevenueCat receives your purchase history and an anonymous app user identifier. We never see your payment details.

Messages to the developer

If you use “Write to us”, your message, the optional contact you type, the app version, device model and language are sent to us so we can reply. Nothing from your diary is attached unless you paste it yourself.

Notifications

The optional evening check-in is a local notification scheduled on your device. No data leaves the phone to show it.

Children

The app is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them.

Deleting your data

Delete any attack from its page, or uninstall the app to remove the whole diary from your device. To delete analytics data tied to your device identifier, write to us at the address below.
