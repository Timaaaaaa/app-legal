Migraine Tracker: Headache Log · Android

Logging an attack

  • Tap “I have a migraine now” on the Today screen, or the “Log” button on the home-screen widget. Intensity and time are enough; everything else is optional and can be added later.
  • When the attack is over, tap “It's over” on the Today screen. The counter of days without migraine starts from there.
  • Forgot to log one? Use “Log a past attack” and set the start and end time.

Widget

Settings → “Add home screen widget”, or long-press the home screen → Widgets → Migraine Tracker. The widget shows days since your last attack and opens the log form in one tap.

Evening check-in

Settings → “Daily check-in”. One notification at the time you choose. Days with no answer count as clear.

Premium

Premium unlocks insights, the report export, full history and custom triggers. If you already paid and Premium shows as off, open Settings → “Restore purchases” on the same Google account you used to buy.

Your data

Everything stays on your phone. Deleting the app deletes the diary. Export it first from Insights → Report → Export CSV.
